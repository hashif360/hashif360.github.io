I've been meaning to start a blog for roughly as long as I've been writing code. There was
always a better use of the afternoon: another feature to ship, another bug to chase, another
idea that felt more urgent than a text box on the internet.

So this is me, finally, choosing the text box.

## Why now

Building products taught me something uncomfortable: I forget things. Hard-won lessons — the
kind that cost a weekend — evaporate within a month, and I end up re-learning them the
expensive way. Writing is how I stop paying twice.

> The palest ink is better than the best memory.

That's the whole thesis of this place. If I write it down, maybe I'll only make each mistake
once.

## What you'll find here

- **Build logs** — what I'm working on, what broke, and how I got out.
- **Craft notes** — small ideas about design, code, and shipping that don't fit in a tweet.
- **Post-mortems** — the honest kind, where I admit what I got wrong.

No content calendar, no growth funnel, no newsletter pop-up. Just notes from someone who
builds things, written mostly for a future version of me who's about to make the same
mistake again.

If a future-you finds them useful too — even better.
