About

Hashif Habeeb

I'm a developer who likes to build the whole thing — from the database to the pixel — and ship it to real people.

Over the years I've started and shipped a handful of products, each one teaching me something new about design, distribution, and the unglamorous work that turns an idea into something people actually use. This blog is where I write those lessons down before I forget them.

I care about fast software, honest interfaces, and shipping. When I'm not building, I'm usually reading about how other people build.
