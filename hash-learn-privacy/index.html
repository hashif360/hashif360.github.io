Hash Learn

Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 2026

The short version

Hash Learn collects no data. There are no accounts, no analytics, no ads, and no network requests of any kind. Everything the app stores — your XP, streak, quiz history, notes, and lesson progress — stays on your device, in the app's own storage, and is never sent anywhere.

What's stored, and where

Your progress, spaced-repetition review schedule, notes, and preferences are saved to your device's local app storage (Android's SharedPreferences on the mobile app, or your browser's local storage on the web version). Uninstalling the app or clearing its storage deletes this data permanently — Hash Learn has no server to recover it from, because it doesn't have a server.

Third parties

Hash Learn does not share data with anyone, because it does not collect any to share.

Children's privacy

Because Hash Learn collects no personal information from anyone, it collects no personal information from children.

Changes to this policy

If this policy ever changes (for example, if a future version adds an online feature), the updated policy will be posted at this address before that version ships.
